# Market Research — TrendPulse

Produce research that supports innovation decisions, not research theater.

## When to Activate

- Sizing a food trend opportunity (TAM/SAM for a category)
- Analyzing competitor activity in a food segment
- Researching consumer behavior shifts (GLP-1, protein, global flavors)
- Evaluating a trend signal for the opportunity brief

## Research Standards

1. Every important claim needs a source.
2. Prefer recent data (< 12 months) and flag stale data.
3. Include contrarian evidence and downside cases.
4. Translate findings into an innovation decision, not just a summary.
5. Separate fact, inference, and recommendation clearly.

## Research Modes

### Trend Opportunity Sizing
- Top-down: Category size from Circana/NIQ or trade reports
- Bottom-up: Google search volume × conversion assumptions
- Analogous benchmark: Compare to a similar trend that already matured (e.g., Nashville Hot → Indian flavors)
- Include confidence range (low/mid/high)

### Competitive Analysis
- Who is already in this space? (brands, SKUs, positioning)
- What's their distribution footprint?
- What are consumers saying about them? (Reddit, reviews)
- Where are the gaps Tyson could fill?

### Consumer Behavior Research
- What's driving the trend? (health, culture, convenience, social media)
- Is this structural (lasting 3+ years) or cyclical?
- Which demographic is leading adoption?
- What's the purchase intent vs. awareness gap?

## Output Format

1. Executive summary (3-5 sentences)
2. Key findings (bulleted, sourced)
3. Implications for Tyson
4. Risks and caveats
5. Recommendation (act/watch/skip)
6. Sources

## Quality Gate

Before delivering:
- All numbers are sourced or labeled as estimates
- Old data is flagged
- The recommendation follows from the evidence
- Risks and counterarguments are included
- The output makes an innovation decision easier
